01 — Brand Overview

WHO WE ARE

Créatif Mediums and Promotions is a Durban-based full-service marketing, promotions, activations, and IT support agency. Founded in KwaZulu-Natal's Musgrave/Berea corridor, we bridge the gap between digital marketing, physical brand activations, and technology support — serving SMEs who need enterprise-quality execution at accessible prices.

We are not just a marketing agency. We maintain a South African Promoter Database, train brand ambassadors, manage events and activations, build websites, run digital campaigns, and provide Apple/IT deployment and end-user support. Our Level 1 BBBEE certification positions us as a preferred supplier for corporate and government contracts.

07 — Usage Rules

DO'S & DON'TS

✓ Do

  • ✓ Always include the period in "Créatif."
  • ✓ Always include the accent: Créatif, not Creatif
  • ✓ Use the Durban skyline icon with the wordmark
  • ✓ Maintain high contrast between text and background
  • ✓ Use Gold on Charcoal or Charcoal on Gold
  • ✓ Include all three service verticals in brand communications
  • ✓ Display BBBEE Level 1 on proposals and marketing
  • ✓ Use the gradient for service info and promotional content

✗ Don't

  • ✗ Never use "Creatif" without the accent mark
  • ✗ Never drop the period from the wordmark
  • ✗ Never stretch, distort, or rotate the logo
  • ✗ Never use Gold text on White (low contrast)
  • ✗ Never present the agency as "just marketing"
  • ✗ Never use light text on the gold/orange portion of the gradient (low contrast)
  • ✗ Never use more than 2 typefaces in a single composition
  • ✗ Never use the skyline icon without the wordmark
